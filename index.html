Our story · Since 1980

More Than a Meal. A Tradition.

The family started with a small bakery on Pico Boulevard and Union — and grew, and grew, until they had taken over two more storefronts on the same corner. Today the tortillería, the panadería and the restaurant all share one roof at 1287 S Union Ave.

Everything is still made the way it was in 1980: fresh corn tortillas, pan dulce from the case, and hot dishes from Nicaragua, Mexico and every country in Central America.

Fresh From Our Panadería

This is where it all started — a small panadería on Pico and Union. More than 45 years later the case still holds panes from all over Latin America, baked fresh every day, plus cakes, flan and fruit tarts.

The one everyone asks for

Tres Leches Cake

The standout of the pastry case — reviewers call it a must-try. Order a whole cake ahead for birthdays and gatherings; the counter can help you plan it.
