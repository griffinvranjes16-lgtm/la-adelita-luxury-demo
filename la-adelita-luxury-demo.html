Our story

Más de 45 años de servicio

The family started with a small bakery on Pico Boulevard and Union — and grew, and grew, until they had taken over two more storefronts on the same corner. Today the tortillería, the panadería and the restaurant all share one roof at 1287 S Union Ave.

Everything is still made the way it was in 1980: fresh corn tortillas, pan dulce from the case, and hot dishes from Mexico and every country in Central America.

Before you come

Open every day, 6 AM

Monday–Saturday 6:00 AM–9:30 PM, Sunday 6:00 AM–9:00 PM. Breakfast, lunch and dinner — and the bakery case is full from the morning.

Walk in — no reservations

Mostly Spanish, all welcome

Most of the signage is in Spanish and the staff mainly speak Spanish — a little basic Spanish goes a long way, and the food speaks for itself.
